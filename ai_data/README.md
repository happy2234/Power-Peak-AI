# ai_data: files, columns, source

Generated by `scripts/export_ai_data.py` (read-only, seed 42, no network). Percent columns are percent; `unit` is MW (peaks) or MU (energy). 12-month window = forecasts issued 2025-11-01 .. 2026-11-07 (Grid-India: target days from 2025-11-01).

Stored-file sources actually used: backtest_processed.parquet <- this project; fixed_split_predictions.parquet <- /home/gauravkumar/projects/india-power-forecast; everything else from this project.

- **model_cards.csv** - columns: model, key, type, architecture, parameters_or_trees, parameter_unit, input_window, encoder_features, decoder_features, distinct_features, feature_set, hidden, layers, d_model, heads, dropout, learning_rate, batch_size, weight_decay, optimiser, scheduler, early_stopping_patience, num_leaves, n_estimators_max, min_child_samples, colsample_bytree, subsample, reg_lambda, max_bin, epochs_run, best_epoch, best_iterations_P50, best_validation_loss, best_validation_loss_unit, training_seconds, device, trained_through, model_dir, extra. Source: models/*/<latest>/config.yaml, history.csv, model.pt/model.pkl; data/processed/training_log.jsonl, postprocess.json; src/models/*.
- **model_cards.json** - columns: one object per model; same fields as the CSV plus feature_list and extra. Source: same as model_cards.csv + full feature lists.
- **model_metrics.csv** - columns: window, model, model_key, target, unit, mape_pct, mae, rmse, bias, bias_pct, pinball_mean, coverage_p10_p90_pct, n_days, n_forecasts. Source: data/processed/backtest_processed.parquet (rolling; origins >= 2025-11-01, Grid-India days >= 2025-11-01) and fixed_split_predictions.parquet (fixed test, from india-power-forecast); Grid-India fixed rows = its backtest_processed rows with date >= 2025-04-01; metrics via src.models.common.metrics.
- **metrics_by_horizon.csv** - columns: model, model_key, target, unit, days_ahead, mape_pct, mae, bias, bias_pct, coverage_p10_p90_pct, n_days. Source: data/processed/backtest_processed.parquet (12-month window; Grid-India omitted: its 'h' counts from its forecast-week start, not from the issue date).
- **predictions_long.csv** - columns: date, target, unit, model, model_key, days_ahead, origin, actual, p10, p50, p90, in_12m_window. Source: data/processed/backtest_processed.parquet (calibrated P10/P90; includes the 2025-05..2025-10 warm-up months, flagged by in_12m_window=False). Only the 6 headline models; variants omitted.
- **error_distribution.csv** - columns: model, model_key, target, n_forecasts, abs_pct_error_p25, abs_pct_error_p50, abs_pct_error_p75, abs_pct_error_p90, abs_pct_error_p95, abs_pct_error_max, mape_pct. Source: data/processed/backtest_processed.parquet (12-month window; one row per forecast = origin x day ahead).
- **worst_days.csv** - columns: model, model_key, target, rank, date, actual, forecast, pct_error, n_forecasts, signed_pct_error, weather_condition, weekday, holiday_festival, unit. Source: backtest_processed.parquet + features.parquet (weather regime via src.evaluate.weather_regimes) + src.features.calendar; forecast = mean P50 over the days-ahead available for that date.
- **errors_by_group.csv** - columns: model, model_key, target, group_type, group, mape_pct, bias_pct, n_days, n_forecasts. Source: backtest_processed.parquet (12-month window) + features.parquet (tmax_ai quartiles over the window's days) + src.evaluate (SEASONS, weather_regimes) + src.features.calendar.
- **ablation.csv** - columns: split_info, variant, target, n_features, test_mape_pct, val_mape_pct, val_change_vs_all_pp, test_change_vs_all_pp, test_change_vs_all_pct. Source: fresh in-memory LightGBM fits (src.models.baselines.LGBMQuantile, compact feature set, hyper-parameters models/lgbm/best_params_fixed_split.yaml), fixed split from src.fixed_split; nothing saved to models/.
- **feature_importance.csv** - columns: target, quantile, feature, group, gain, pct_of_total. Source: models/lgbm/<latest>/model.pkl (LightGBM gain, 9 models: 3 targets x P10/P50/P90).
- **feature_importance_by_group.csv** - columns: target, quantile, group, n_features, gain, pct_of_total. Source: sum of feature_importance.csv per group (and mean over the 3 quantiles).
- **lstm_permutation_importance.csv** - columns: level, name, group, target, baseline_mape_pct, permuted_mape_pct, increase_pp, in_encoder, in_decoder, n_validation_origins, validation_origins. Source: models/lstm/<latest> (production LSTM) on its validation slice (last 15 % of origins); input columns shuffled across samples (seed 42, 5 repeats per feature, 10 per group); metric = MAPE of P50.
- **lstm_permutation_importance_by_group.csv** - columns: group, target, sum_of_single_feature_increase_pp, joint_permutation_increase_pp. Source: lstm_permutation_importance.csv (sum of single-feature increases vs permuting the whole group at once).
- **tft_variable_selection.csv** - columns: side, feature, group, mean_selection_weight, pct_of_side_total, validation_origins. Source: models/tft/<latest> variable-selection softmax weights (mean over validation origins and time steps).
- **tft_attention.csv** - columns: days_ahead, attended_position, days_back_from_origin, mean_attention_weight. Source: models/tft/<latest> attention weights (head-averaged, mean over validation origins).
- **ensemble_vs_members.csv** - columns: target, days_ahead, lstm_mape_pct, lightgbm_mape_pct, ensemble_mape_pct, ensemble_gain_vs_best_member_pp. Source: data/processed/backtest_processed.parquet (12-month window).
- **ensemble_weights.csv** - columns: target, days_ahead, w_lstm, w_lightgbm, w_lstm_fixed_split. Source: data/processed/postprocess.json ('ensemble': live weights fitted on the last 180 days of backtest rows) and fixed_split_info.json ('post': weights fitted on the fixed-split validation).
- **calibration.csv** - columns: model, model_key, target, days_ahead, unit, coverage_before_pct, coverage_after_pct, width_before, width_after, target_coverage_pct, n_forecasts, width_change_pct. Source: data/processed/backtest_processed.parquet (p10_raw/p90_raw = before, p10/p90 = after per-horizon adaptive conformal calibration; 12-month window).
- **training_curves.csv** - columns: model, epoch, train_loss, val_loss, learning_rate, epoch_seconds, refit_loss_train_plus_val, is_best_epoch, loss_unit, source_dir. Source: models/lstm/<latest>/history.csv and models/tft/<latest>/history.csv.
- **lightgbm_training_curve.csv** - columns: model, target, quantile, iteration, train_loss, val_loss, is_best_iter, loss_unit, fit_window. Source: NOT stored by the project; recomputed in memory with the same fixed split and hyper-parameters as ablation.csv (all features). Not the production model.
- **model_ladder.csv** - columns: step, model, model_key, solar_peak_mape_pct, evening_peak_mape_pct, energy_mape_pct, overall_mape_pct, overall_change_vs_previous_step_pp, improves_on_previous_step, rank_overall (1=best). Source: model_metrics.csv (rolling 12-month backtest), steps in the requested order.
- **worked_example_2026-11-14.csv** - columns: section, name, date, days_ahead, target, model, value (raw / actual where known), unit, scaled_value, scaler_mean, scaler_sd, p10, p50, p90, source. Source: features.parquet, make_tabular/make_sequences (src.features.build), models/lstm/<latest>/scaler.npz, forecast_history.parquet, latest_forecast.parquet.
